01About Mahan

Packaging is the quiet infrastructure of trade.

Every box that leaves our Nairobi plant carries somebody’s product, reputation and promise to a customer. We build it the way we would want it built for us — accurate to the millimetre, strong enough for the journey, and honest about what it costs.

Vision · 01

To be East Africa’s most trusted packaging partner — protecting the products that move the region’s economy, and setting the standard for sustainable corrugated packaging.

Mission · 02

To design and manufacture strong, reliable and affordable corrugated packaging from our base in Nairobi, helping growers, exporters, manufacturers and online retailers get their products to market safely, on time and with less waste.

02What we offer

Board, built to your spec.

Corrugated boxes in the ply, colour and print your product actually needs — from light retail cartons to heavy export shippers, all made under one roof in Nairobi.

3-ply corrugated boxes

Clean, light-board cartons for retail-ready goods — cosmetics, confectionery, herbs and everyday online orders that need to arrive crisp and undamaged.

  • White or brown board
  • Retail and e-commerce ready
  • Clean die-cut finishing

5-ply corrugated boxes

Double-wall strength for concentrated weight, tall stacks and long distribution chains — bottles, paint tins, flower exports and palletised freight.

  • Double-wall construction
  • High stacking strength
  • Export and transit tested

Custom printing

Print your brand, handling marks and product story directly onto the carton — so every box that leaves your line works as shelf presence, not just protection.

  • Single and multi-colour print
  • Brand, message and design
  • Consistent ink coverage
